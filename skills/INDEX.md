# Area skills

Plain folders, copied from the direction kit's skill library (`~/.claude/kit-skills/`).
A card names at most three under its "Skills" heading; only those are read for that card.

| Area    | Skill                              | When to use it                                                                                           | Source                          |
| ------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| quality | [deslop](quality/deslop/)          | Taking slop out of a card's diff (narrating comments, one-use helpers, casts) without changing behaviour | kit library, written for the kit |

`deslop` is always copied, because `/done` runs it. No other areas were picked yet: the
owner chooses which of the library's areas (docs, legal, motion, quality, testing, ui,
workflow) this repo needs, and `/setup` copies them here.
