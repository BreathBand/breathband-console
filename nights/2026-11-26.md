## Decided for the owner (overnight)

The calls worth a look. Every card's Decisions section has the full list.

- **Calibration (T-0101):** the simulated band breathes 2.5 s in and 3 s out, five times. The reference is still the Appendix's mean of the top and bottom three.
- **Age (T-0103):** asked on the sign-up form, and year-based: someone counts as 16 from 1 January of the year they turn 16.
- **Streak (T-0113):** a day counts if it has any session (the alternative was "ring closed"); one automatic rest day per rolling 7 days; the ring defaults to the goal's session length (Train 10 / Focus 3 / Calm 3 min, where the spec says 5); milestones for Calm only.
- **First session (T-0115):** Train's first session is 3 minutes, and it goes straight to the instrument.
- **Auth (T-0111):** sign-in is limited to 5 attempts per 60 s per IP, held in memory; email verification is a wall, not a banner (an open question).
- **API (T-0117):** `birthYear` and consents are returned only to the person themselves; 256 KB body limit (6 MB for sessions).
- **Coach invites (T-0121, T-0122):** athlete invite codes are stored as sha256, expire in 7 days and redeem once; coach invite codes are derived as HMAC(AUTH_SECRET, …), so rotating AUTH_SECRET breaks pending links.
- **Mood (T-0119):** check-ins are a private list that coaches can't read, saved with the session; local DB v8 → v9 migrates in place.
- **Programs (T-0124):** a missed day waits (no reset); a finished program stays on Home for 7 days; **the 14-day Foundations content is a draft for Harry**.
- **Account deletion (T-0126):** a claimed request reads as "requested at 1970" until the job finishes (the alternative is a `claimed_at` column).
- **Lead deviation (T-0119):** let the new `MoodScore` / `MoodCheckin` types through as additive, although they weren't on the approved list. T-0123 was held for the same kind of change.
