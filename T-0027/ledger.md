- 2026-11-24 06:14 2. Create src/lib/analysis/filter.ts as pure TypeScript with no dependency. butterworthLowPass(sampleRateHz) reads cutoffHz and order from FIXED_CONSTANTS.lowPassFilter, pre-warps with tan(π fc / fs), builds the analog Butterworth poles, applies the bilinear transform and returns cascaded second-order sections normalised to unity DC gain. It throws a RangeError when the cutoff is at or above Nyquist. filtfilt(samples, sampleRateHz) applies the sections forward and then backward. It pads each edge with an odd reflection about the end sample (length set by the filter order, clamped for short inputs) and sets steady-state initial conditions so edges don't ring. It returns an array the same length as the input.
- 2026-11-24 06:14 3. Write src/lib/analysis/filter.test.ts. At 20 Hz and 10 Hz, filter 60 s sines at 0.1, 0.5, 1 and 3 Hz. Measure amplitude over the middle of the signal by least-squares fit or RMS ratio, and compare it with 1 / (1 + (tan(π f / fs) / tan(π fc / fs))^8): within 1% relative, or within 1e-4 absolute where the analytic gain is below 0.01. Also check that the peak indices of a filtered 0.1 Hz sine are within one sample of the unfiltered ones, and that the output length equals the input length.
- 2026-11-24 06:14 4. Create src/lib/analysis/transitions.ts and export DetectedTransition {index, t, type: 'peak' | 'trough', level}. Steps: (1) mark samples with |x| > FIXED_CONSTANTS.artifactLimitPctOfMax as artifacts and fill them by linear interpolation from the neighbouring good samples, without removing any; (2) run filtfilt; (3) find interior local maxima and minima, treating a flat plateau as one candidate at its middle, never at the array edges and never on artifact samples; (4) keep candidates whose topographic prominence is at least the threshold; (5) for same-type candidates closer than OPEN_CONSTANTS.transitionMinSpacingS (converted to samples), keep the more extreme one; (6) enforce alternation by collapsing runs of the same type to the highest peak or lowest trough; (7) drop any extremum with |level| > artifactLimitPctOfMax. Level is the filtered value and t = index / sampleRateHz. Export detectTransitions(pctSamples, sampleRateHz, options?: {minProminence}) and a lower-level helper that calibration reuses. The default prominence is OPEN_CONSTANTS.transitionMinProminencePctOfMax. No literals and no comments.

It takes the calibrationPeakCount highest peak levels and the calibrationTroughCount lowest trough levels. a100 is the difference of their means and midline is the average of the two means. It returns null when fewer peaks or troughs than those counts are found, or when a100 ≤ 0. toPctOfMax(samples, calibration) maps (x − M) / A100 × 100. calibrationFromLegacy({min, max}) returns {a100: max − min, midline: (max + min) / 2, peaks: [], troughs: [], approximate: true}.
